# 贡献者行为准则 | Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## 中文版（简化）

为营造开放友好的社区环境，参与 MaxKB4j 项目（Issue、PR、讨论、社区群等一切渠道）时请遵守：

- **保持友善与尊重**：不进行人身攻击、贬低性评论或针对性挑衅
- **聚焦技术本身**：对事不对人，批评代码但不批评人
- **欢迎新手**： patiently 回答初学者问题，营造无门槛的参与氛围
- **不做推广与无关内容**：不在 Issue/PR 中发布广告、拉票等无关内容
- **遵守法律与许可**：尊重 GPLv3 许可证及第三方知识产权

不可接受的行为包括：骚扰、歧视性言论（涉及性别、种族、宗教、国籍等）、发布他人隐私、持续纠缠等。

违反准则的内容将被删除，情节严重者将被移出社区。如需举报，请通过 [Gitee Issues](https://gitee.com/taisan/MaxKB4j/issues) 或联系作者。

## English (summary)

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include: using welcoming and inclusive language, being respectful of differing viewpoints, gracefully accepting constructive criticism, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include: the use of sexualized language or unwelcome attention, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct considered unethical or unprofessional in a professional setting.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may ban contributors who repeatedly engage in unacceptable behavior. Report issues via [Gitee Issues](https://gitee.com/taisan/MaxKB4j/issues).

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) version 2.1.
